The Virtuous Human

You become what you do every day.

Not what you believe, or plan, or mean to get round to. What you actually do, on an ordinary Tuesday, when nobody's watching.

Every day is a vote for who you're turning into.
Skip enough of them and you lose the vote.

On purpose, or by default.

You're becoming someone either way. Most of us drift into it, doing whatever's easiest. We're building three small apps that help you do it on purpose: they show you what you really did, so the gap between who you think you are and who you're becoming can't hide.

Phase

For your body. iPhone, with Apple Health.

  • Take a photo of what you eat. That's the logging done.
  • See how the day really went: what you ate, what you burned, whether you moved.
  • Got DEXA scans? See what's actually changing, fat and muscle, not just the number on the scale.
  • Tell it who you're trying to become. If a week slides, it says so, and shows you where that road goes.

The person you'll be at seventy is being made this week.

It starts tomorrow morning. And the morning after that.
