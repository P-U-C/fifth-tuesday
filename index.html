The Virtuous Human

You become what you do every day.

Not what you believe, or plan, or mean to get round to. What you actually do, on an ordinary Tuesday, when it doesn’t feel like it matters and nobody is watching. Your body keeps that record whether you look at it or not.

Each day is a small vote for the person you’re turning into. One vote changes nothing. A year of them changes everything, and most of us only find out how we voted when it’s too late to change it.

A year of days. The ones you kept, kept adding up.

· · ·

On purpose, or by default

You’re becoming someone either way. The only question is whether you choose it, or drift into whatever was easiest. We’re making three small apps to help you choose: one for your body, one for your head, one for your money. They don’t lecture. They show you what you really did, so the gap between who you think you are and who you’re becoming has nowhere to hide.

Phase

Phase is for your body, on iPhone, with Apple Health. Take a photo of what you eat and that’s the logging done. It knows what you burned, so you can see how the day really went. If you have DEXA scans, it uses them to show what’s actually changing, fat and muscle, rather than the number on the scale. Tell it who you’re trying to become, and when a week slides it will say so, and show you where that road leads.
