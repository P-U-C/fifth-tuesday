The Virtuous Human

Most of a good life is small stuff, done often.

We're making three apps to help with it: one for your body, one for your head, one for your money. Phase is the first.

Phase

For iPhone. Works with Apple Health.

  • Take a photo of your lunch. Phase works out what's in it.
  • It knows what you've burned, so you can see where the day stands.
  • Got DEXA scans? It uses them to show what's actually changing, fat and muscle, not just weight.
  • Tell it what you're going for. If a week goes sideways, it'll let you know.
