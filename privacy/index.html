Privacy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Phase is an iPhone app for the body: food against burn, training, and body composition. P-U-C makes it. This policy says what Phase does with your information. In short: your data stays on your iPhone, and P-U-C never receives it.

What Phase keeps, and where

  • On your iPhone only. Your meals, DEXA scans, workout templates and sessions, targets and settings are stored in Phase's own storage on your iPhone. They are not sent to P-U-C. They are included in your iPhone's own backups.
  • Apple Health. With your permission, Phase reads from Apple Health: active and resting energy, exercise minutes, workouts, weight, body fat and lean mass, sleep, heart rate variability, swimming distance and nutrition other apps logged. It writes the meals you log (energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat) and the strength sessions you finish. You choose what Phase may read and write in the Health app, and can change it at any time. Phase does not use Health data for advertising or marketing, and never sells it.
  • Your Claude API key, if you add one, is kept in your iPhone's Keychain.

What leaves your iPhone, only when you ask

  • Claude (Anthropic). When you log food with a photo, your voice or words, or import a DEXA report, Phase sends that photo, those words (voice is turned into text on your iPhone first) or that PDF to Anthropic's Claude API, using your own API key, and gets the estimate back. Phase asks before the first time, and you can turn it off in Settings › Claude. Anthropic handles it under your agreement with Anthropic and its privacy policy (anthropic.com/legal/privacy). Phase sends nothing else to Anthropic: no Health data, no history.
  • Your own connector (optional, advanced). If you set up a Claude connector you host yourself, Phase sends it a summary of your recent days, meals, body estimate and training so that Claude can read them, and receives what Claude logs for you. That server is yours; P-U-C has no access to it.
  • Exports you share. Settings › Export your data writes a file of your days on your iPhone. It goes only where you send it.

What P-U-C receives

Nothing. Phase has no P-U-C account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. If you contact P-U-C for support, we see only what you send us.

Children

Phase is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Delete Phase from your iPhone to remove everything it stored. Meals and sessions Phase wrote to Apple Health stay in Health until you delete them there (Health › Browse, or Health › your profile › Apps › Phase). To revoke Claude access, delete your key in Phase › Settings › Claude and, if you like, revoke it at platform.claude.com.

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if this changes, with a new date at the top. Questions: Phase support.
