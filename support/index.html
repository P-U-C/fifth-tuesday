Logging food with Claude

Phase reads a photo or a few words with Claude, using your own Claude API key. Make one at platform.claude.com, then paste it into Phase › Settings › Claude. It stays in your iPhone's Keychain. Anthropic bills your key directly: about a cent a meal. Without a key, enter meals by hand.

Apple Health

Phase reads burn, exercise, workouts, weight, body fat, lean mass, sleep and food other apps logged, and writes the meals you log and the strength sessions you finish. To change what it may use: the Health app › your profile › Apps › Phase.

DEXA scans

Settings › Import a DEXA report reads the PDF with Claude; you check every number before it's saved. Or add a scan by hand: the date and body fat are enough.

Your data

Everything stays on your iPhone. Settings › Export your data gives you a JSON and a CSV of your days to keep or to share. Deleting Phase removes what it stored; what it wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in Health.
