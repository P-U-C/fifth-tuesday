Support

Something broken or missing? It's a beta, so tell us: open an issue. Describe what happened; don't paste health data.

How do I log food with a photo?

Phase reads a photo or a few words with Claude, using your own Claude API key. Make one at platform.claude.com and paste it into Phase › Settings › Claude. It stays in your iPhone's Keychain, and Anthropic bills it directly: about a cent a meal. Phase asks before the first time anything is sent. Without a key, enter meals by hand.

What does Phase use from Apple Health?

Burn, exercise, workouts, weight, body fat, lean mass, sleep, and food other apps logged. It writes the meals you log and the strength sessions you finish. Change what it may use in the Health app › your profile › Apps › Phase.

How do DEXA scans work?

Settings › Import a DEXA report reads the PDF with Claude, and you check every number before it's saved. Or add a scan by hand: the date and body fat are enough. Scans keep the body fat and muscle numbers honest.

What is Intention?

Settings › Intention: maintain, lose fat, gain muscle, or lose fat and gain muscle together. Phase checks the last seven days against it. When you drift, the screen's edge glows and Today shows where that road leads.

Where is my data, and can I take it with me?

Everything stays on your iPhone. Settings › Export your data gives you a JSON and a CSV of your days. Deleting Phase removes what it stored; what it wrote to Apple Health stays there until you delete it in Health. More in the privacy policy.
